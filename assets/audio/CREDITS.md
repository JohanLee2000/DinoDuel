# Audio credits

Sound effects in `sfx/` are from Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain;
no attribution required, credited anyway). Packs: Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, Music Jingles,
Casino Audio, RPG Audio. License text: `KENNEY_LICENSE.txt`.

`bite_*`, `brace_*` and `charge_*` are original, made by `tools/synth_sfx.py` (synthesis layered
with CC0 Kenney impacts from `tools/sfx_sources/`). `tap` and `clutch_open` are Jo's.

Music in `music/` (`main.ogg`, `battle.ogg`) was generated by Jo with an AI music tool on a plan that allows commercial use, then trimmed into seamless loops.
