Dino Duel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Dino Duel mobile game ("the game"), published on Google Play by Dino Duel Studios, handles information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information the game collects

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs. It does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (your dino collection, Amber, eggs, party and battle record) is saved in the game's private storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Google Play

When you download the game, Google Play handles the download and may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive personal information about you from Google.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds features that handle information differently (for example, optional in-app purchases through Google Play), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
