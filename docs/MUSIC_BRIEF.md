# Music brief

Two looping instrumental tracks. Jo generates them with an AI music tool **on a plan that allows commercial use** (keep a note of the plan and date in case Google or the tool ever asks). Upload the results in chat; I'll trim them to loop cleanly, convert to `.ogg` and drop them in `assets/audio/music/`. The game already plays them by file name, so nothing else changes.

| File | Plays on | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `main.ogg` | Tabs (Battle list, Party, Eggs, Dex) and the pick screen | 90-150 s |
| `battle.ogg` | Battles | 90-150 s |

Tips for any tool:
- Turn on **Instrumental** (no vocals).
- Ask for no intro build-up, no fade-out and no big ending, so the track loops. A steady middle section loops best.
- Generate a few versions of each and pick the one that sits under the sound effects without fighting them (not too busy in the high end).
